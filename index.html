ABOUT

About Me

I am a Master in Public Policy candidate at the Lee Kuan Yew School of Public Policy, National University of Singapore, with professional and research experience spanning education technology, public policy, human rights, and forced migration in Southeast Asia.

My professional background is rooted in education and technology. Since 2019, I have worked at 360Ed, where I have managed digital education products and cross-functional projects serving more than 350,000 users. My work has involved product and project management, monitoring and evaluation, digital content, and collaboration with government and international partners. Alongside this, I have worked on educator development and digital learning initiatives, including the development of an e-learning programme and an online community supporting educators from Myanmar.

My academic and research interests increasingly focus on refugee protection, migration, human rights, civilian protection, and regional policy in Southeast Asia. Through research and fellowship experiences with CRPI, ALTSEAN-Burma, the Refugee Studies Centre at the University of Oxford, and other institutions, I have worked on participatory research, human rights documentation, atrocity prevention, and refugee protection. I am particularly interested in how public policy and regional institutions respond to displacement and other challenges affecting communities in conflict-affected contexts.
