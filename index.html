My professional background is rooted in education and technology. Since 2019, I have worked at 360Ed, where I have managed digital education products and cross-functional projects serving more than 350,000 users. My work has involved product and project management, monitoring and evaluation, digital content, and collaboration with government and international partners.

Alongside my professional work, my academic and research interests increasingly focus on refugee protection, migration, human rights, civilian protection, and regional policy in Southeast Asia. Through research and fellowship experiences with CRPI, ALTSEAN-Burma, the Refugee Studies Centre at the University of Oxford, and other institutions, I have worked on participatory research, human rights documentation, atrocity prevention, and refugee protection.
